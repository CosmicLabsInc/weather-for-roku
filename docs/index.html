Privacy Policy — Weather for Roku

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Weather is a simple app that shows a 7-day forecast for a U.S. ZIP code.

We do not collect any data

We collect nothing. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server operated by us. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

The 5-digit ZIP code you enter is saved only locally on your own Roku device so the app can show your forecast without asking again. It never comes to us. You can change it any time by pressing *.

How the forecast is fetched

Your ZIP code is converted to an approximate location entirely on your device using a bundled public-domain lookup table — no geocoding service is contacted. The app then requests the forecast for that approximate location from the U.S. National Weather Service API (api.weather.gov), a free public service of the U.S. government.

That request is made directly from your device to the National Weather Service and contains only the approximate coordinates needed to return a forecast. We operate no server and receive no copy of this data.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
